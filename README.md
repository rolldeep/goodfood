# goodfood

**Good Food Runbook** (`goodfood-runbook.html`): an interactive runbook for improving eating habits.

1. **Triage**: answer 11 questions about the last two weeks (Rarely / Sometimes / Often).
2. **Plays**: your answers rank 11 plays as P1 (fix first), P2 (next) or OK. Each play has a *Do today* action, a Tiny Habits recipe ("After I…, I will…"), a number to track, and a source.
3. **Weekly review**: a 15-minute Sunday loop to keep, graduate or shrink each play, plus guidance on when to see a professional.

Advice draws on Bryan Johnson (Blueprint), Michael Pollan, Peter Attia, BJ Fogg, WHO guidelines, and the NIH ultra-processed food trial (Hall et al. 2019).

**Daily companion** (`companion/`): a private Telegram bot that walks you through Andrew Huberman's three basics (morning light, resistance training, sleep) with voice notes, and saves every reply. See [companion/README.md](companion/README.md).

General education, not medical advice.
